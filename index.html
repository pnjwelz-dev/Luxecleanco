Meet Peg & Deb

The Heart Behind Luxe Signature & Co.

Peg and Deb met while working as caregivers in a small assisted living home. They became friends quickly, then best friends, and built Luxe around the kind of practical help they’ve always been glad to give.

Meet Peg & Deb — the women behind the signature.

What began as a friendship became something much more meaningful: a vision for a company built around beauty, excellence, trust, and care.

For Peg and Deb, luxury has never been about excess. It is about the details others overlook. It is the feeling of walking into a beautifully cared-for home. The fresh scent in the air. The perfectly finished surface. The sense that, for a moment, everything is exactly where it belongs.

That is the experience they set out to create.

Family is at the heart of both women. Peg is a mother whose life revolves around her children and the beautiful, sometimes wonderfully chaotic reality of family life. Deb shares that same devotion, with her grandchildren holding a treasured place in her world.

Those values naturally became part of LSC.

They believe your home should be more than clean — it should feel cared for. Your business should make the right impression. Your vehicle should feel refreshed. And when life becomes overwhelming, asking for help should never feel like something to be ashamed of.

So they created a company that brings together refined service and genuine heart.
